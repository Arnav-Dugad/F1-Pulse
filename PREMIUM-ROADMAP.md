# F1 Pulse premium roadmap

These 60 ideas are future additions. The desktop/mobile polish, page finder, saved compact/calm preferences and Cloudflare deployment are implemented separately. Live data ideas require available provider coverage; analytical models should expose assumptions and uncertainty.

## Advanced race features

1. Multi-driver telemetry workspace with linked charts and shared cursors.
2. Race replay timeline with lap-by-lap scrubbing and event markers.
3. Pit-stop strategy sandbox with editable tyre life and pit-loss assumptions.
4. Undercut/overcut opportunity explorer with estimated rejoin positions.
5. Championship scenario calculator with remaining-round permutations.
6. Spoiler-free mode that conceals results until the viewer opts in.
7. Personal race dashboard pinned to favourite drivers and teams.
8. Opt-in race-weekend reminders with configurable lead times.
9. Watch-party rooms with synchronized replay positions.
10. Driver comparison workspaces that can be saved and shared.
11. Historical race archive with unified filters and comparison views.
12. Race-weekend itinerary with local session times and calendar integration.
13. Fantasy lineup builder with transparent scoring and budget constraints.
14. Prediction leagues with private groups and season leaderboards.
15. Shareable race briefing cards containing verified statistics.

30. Offline weekend packs containing the schedule, track notes and cached results.

## Suggested delivery order

Start with freshness badges, spoiler-free mode, favourite drivers and shareable comparison views. Follow with race replay, telemetry workspaces, stint timelines and scenario calculators. Add immersive motion after the underlying data flow and mobile interactions are reliable.
